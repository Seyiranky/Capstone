# Usability test: 6–11 November 2026 (n = 20)

Evidence for Section 4.5.7 of the technical report (Figure 4.20, Table 4.7).

## What was run

A short Google Form, "Isoko usability test", completed by 20 people after they
opened the live app (https://isoko-web.onrender.com) on their own phone or
computer. The first question was a required consent item; all 20 chose "I agree".
The form collects no names or email addresses.

The test ran under ALU Research Ethics Committee approval **SEPT26-BSE-003**
(11 September 2026). That approval covers this usability test only; the
26-person pilot in Appendix A of the report changes the method and needs a new
approval before it starts.

## What it measured, and what it did not

- **One** ease-of-use question, rated 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy).
- It is **not** the ten-item System Usability Scale (SUS). The mean of 4.9 out of 5
  must not be read as a SUS score or compared with the pilot's SUS target of 68.
- Willingness to hire (RQ3) was not measured.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Participants | 20: 14 clients, 6 workers |
| Reached the sign-in page | 20 of 20 |
| Signed in or registered "easily" | 20 of 20 |
| Ease-of-use rating | eighteen gave 5, two gave 4; mean 4.9 |
